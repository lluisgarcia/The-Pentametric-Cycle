## 2026-10-19 — "Eight Origins" and the Spiral Workbench

The eight origins (±0.3, ±0.1), (±0.1, ±0.3) on the circle of radius √0.1 about the centre of the unit
square, one per symmetry of the Fibonacci tiling; at each one, Holden's two lines are the radius and the
tangent of that circle. Spiral Workbench: an interactive page drawing the eight Fibonacci spirals with their
squares, Holden lines, grids and crossings, with construction and measuring tools.
