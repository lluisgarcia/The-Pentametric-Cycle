The spiral and Holden's lines

The centres of the Fibonacci squares lie on two perpendicular lines that meet at P = (1/10, 3/10), and the centre of the square of side Fk is exactly Lk steps of √0.1 from P (Holden, 1975). The square through P centred on each Fibonacci square has area Fk2 ± 4/5.

Odd-numbered centres lie on y = 3x, even-numbered on x + 3y = 1. Every square Sk is tilted by the same angle arctan(1/2) ≈ 26.57°; with Holden's angle arctan(1/3) it adds up to 45°, and the doubles of the two angles are the angles of the 3–4–5 triangle.

Lines and distances: H. L. Holden, Fibonacci tiles, Fibonacci Quarterly 13 (1975) 45–49. See also T. Gardiner, Note 68.15, Mathematical Gazette (1984).
