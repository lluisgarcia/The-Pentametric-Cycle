Type any whole number and follow the seven steps that take it to an area, a residue and a musical interval. At the Lucas numbers the area is always a perfect square: the square of the matching Fibonacci number.

Which areas are perfect squares?

Proved in the paper: for n ≥ 1, P(n) is a perfect square exactly when n is a Lucas number.
