The Pentametric Cycle

𝔓(n)♪(n)
𝔓(n) = n² + 4 C(m)5, m = 4n² mod 10, C(m) = 26m − 5m²24
𝔓(Lk) = Fk²

The formula as found. C(m) equals the Legendre symbol n5 (0 or ±1, not a fraction), so 𝔓(n) = (n² + 4·n5) / 5. ♪(n) is the interval of 𝔓(n) mod 5.

A formula found by experiment on the squares of the Fibonacci spiral. At every Lucas number 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, 18, … it returns the square of the matching Fibonacci number, and nowhere else is it a perfect square. Reduced modulo 5, it becomes a palindrome of the four Pythagorean consonances: unison, octave, fifth and fourth.
