The chord wheel

Put the twelve notes on the twelve places of a quarter of the palindrome, and every chord becomes a polygon inside the wheel, as on the circle of fifths. The inner palindrome (24 places) and the whole palindrome (50 places) hold two and four octaves of twelve, so there the chords get voices. Here you can choose how the notes are laid out, build any chord, hear it, measure its shape, and see which values of the palindrome lie under its notes. Which note goes on which place is a choice, not something the formula gives, so this page is an exploration.
