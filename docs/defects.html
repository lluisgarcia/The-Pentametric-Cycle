The ±0.8 correction

A square of side n·√0.2 has area n²/5, which is almost never a whole number. The Pentametric Formula moves that area by exactly +0.8, −0.8 or 0 to reach the integer P(n), and the matching circles move by exactly ±2π/5. Which way it moves is decided by the last digit of n.

The last-digit rule

The sign comes from the last digit of the double square 2n² (2, 8, 18, 32, 50, …): it ends in 2 for +0.8, in 8 for −0.8 and in 0 for no correction. This is the Legendre symbol n5 in disguise.
