Ten forms of one formula

The same areas were reached in the spreadsheets by ten different routes: through digits, floors, a polynomial, cosines, a golden rotation and the angles of the pentagon. Each route is a different way of computing the Legendre symbol (n/5), so all ten always agree. Choose n and watch them agree.
