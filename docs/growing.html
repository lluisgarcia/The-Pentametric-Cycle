The growing spiral

Watch the Fibonacci spiral build itself, one square at a time, while the view pulls back. Each new square of side Fk sounds the interval of its Lucas number, H(Lk) = P(Lk) mod 5 = Fk2 mod 5.

Square —

At the Lucas numbers the area is a perfect square, Fk2, and a square leaves remainder 0, 1 or 4 on division by 5. So the spiral only ever sounds silence, the unison or the fourth, in a pattern that repeats every 10 squares: 1, 1, 4, 4, 0, 4, 4, 1, 1, 0.
