The playable palindrome

Reduce P(n) modulo 5 and read 1, 2, 3, 4 as the ratios 1:1, 2:1, 3:2, 4:3 of the tetractys and 0 as silence. The 51 values for n = 0 … 50 form a palindrome around a silent axis, with the fifth at the centre of each half. Press play to hear it in just intonation.

Each period holds 9 silences and 16 = 4² consonances, 9 + 16 = 25 (echoing 3² + 4² = 5²). Its consonances multiply to 2⁸: exactly eight octaves, because a fifth and a fourth make an octave.
