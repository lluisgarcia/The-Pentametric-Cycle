Tangent geometry explorer

Choose a tangent value T. The unit circle stays put while the triangle, the angle and every trig segment rebuild around it.

Normalization. All lengths shown here are normalized so that the hypotenuse is 1. In this construction, the radius/adjacent length is therefore cos(θ), and the opposite length is sin(θ).

The large circle is drawn at radius 1, which is the hypotenuse. The dashed violet circle has radius cos(θ): that is the “Radius” of this model, and it ends at the foot A of the triangle. Bars drawn beside the ray (secant, cosecant, radius) are offset dimension lines, so they never hide the geometry.
