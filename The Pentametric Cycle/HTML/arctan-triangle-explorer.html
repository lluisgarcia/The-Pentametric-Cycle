The same triangle from both acute corners

θ + φ = 90°, for every x. The two acute angles of a right triangle always share one right angle.

Left: the triangle with adjacent = 1 (blue) and opposite = x (red). The dashed φ at the corner is a copy of the far corner's angle: parallel lines make them equal, so θ and φ fill the right angle together. Right: the identical triangle flipped, so the red leg is now adjacent and the blue leg is opposite. The faint arc is a unit circle (radius = the green hypotenuse). Colors follow the physical side, never the trig name.
